## Business Model

### Core Activities

- Design, manufacture, and maintain low-cost underwater drone swarms and bridge command units
- Develop and continuously improve detection AI and swarm coordination software
- Manage fleet logistics: deployment planning, drone replacement, and field support for leased swarms

### Key Partners

- **Sensor and component suppliers** — provide sonar transducers, acoustic modems, and embedded compute modules at volume pricing
- **Maritime compliance and classification societies** (e.g., DNV, Lloyd's Register) — certify hardware for commercial vessel use and unlock insurance premium reductions
- **Marine insurers** — partner to offer premium incentives for Aegis-equipped vessels, creating a financial pull that drives adoption

### Major Costs

- **Drone manufacturing and component procurement** — sonar hardware, housings, electronics, and assembly at scale
- **Engineering salaries** — embedded firmware, AI/ML, acoustic signal processing, and software teams
- **Field trials and maritime compliance** — controlled water tests, certification processes, and pilot deployment logistics

### Revenue Model

- **Monthly lease per vessel ($8K–15K)** — covers drone swarm, bridge command unit, detection software, maintenance, and replacements
- **Annual contracts with monthly billing** — predictable recurring revenue; customers stay because hazard detection is needed every transit
- **Software improvement flywheel** — more deployments yield better detection models, fewer false positives, and higher retention over time

### Recap

- **Value:** Real-time 2 km underwater perimeter around commercial vessels — built-in redundancy, flat monthly lease, no specialized crew needed
- **Problem solved:** Ships are blind to lateral submerged hazards; one grounding costs billions — Aegis makes continuous hazard scouting affordable enough to actually deploy
- **Audience:** Fleet operators and marine insurance risk officers at global shipping conglomerates (Maersk, MSC, Euronav), cruise lines (Carnival, Royal Caribbean), and luxury yacht insurers
