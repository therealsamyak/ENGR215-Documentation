## General Dynamics — Knifefish UUV

### Product Features

- Mine-hunting UUV built for buried-mine detection in cluttered littoral waters
- Carries advanced synthetic aperture sonar optimized for low-visibility seabed classification

### Strengths

- Navy-grade survivability and certification — battle-tested in military operations
- Proven in high-clutter, shallow-water environments where false positives are critical

### Weaknesses

- Defense-first product — not commercially available or priced for shipping operators
- Single-unit architecture with no swarm redundancy; loss ends the mission

## Kongsberg — HUGIN AUV

### Product Features

- Deep-sea survey platform with multi-sensor payload bays (sonar, camera, CTD)
- Designed for long-endurance autonomous seabed mapping missions

### Strengths

- Industry-leading high-resolution survey data — proven in offshore oil & gas
- Mature commercial platform with decades of field deployments and trust

### Weaknesses

- $44M+ contract scale — radically incompatible with routine commercial shipping budgets
- Survey-focused workflow: collect, retrieve, download — no real-time hazard alerting

## General Dynamics / Bluefin Robotics — Bluefin-21

### Product Features

- Side-scan sonar and deep-water endurance for sustained submerged operations
- Heavy payload capacity supporting multi-sensor and modular mission configurations

### Strengths

- Long mission duration — capable of extended deep-water autonomous runs
- Modular architecture with proven track record in salvage and UXO removal

### Weaknesses

- Large single-asset platform — one unit lost means the entire mission fails
- $500K+ price point makes operators hesitant to deploy in the hazards they bought it to scout

## Exail — A18-M AUV

### Product Features

- Mid-size autonomous vehicle purpose-built for mine countermeasure (MCM) operations
- Equipped with high-resolution sonar and autonomous threat-classification software

### Strengths

- Strong MCM focus with modern naval platform — actively deployed by NATO navies
- Mid-size form factor is more deployable than larger deep-sea AUVs

### Weaknesses

- Navy-focused sales channel — no commercial shipping go-to-market or pricing
- Single-unit design, not swarm-native — no distributed redundancy or real-time relay architecture
