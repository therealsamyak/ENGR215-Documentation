## Post-Launch Strategy

- **Insurer co-marketing channel** — once a P&I club or hull underwriter validates the system, they become a distribution channel by featuring Aegis in their recommended safety technology lists to member fleets — credibility we cannot buy
- **Pilot-to-fleet expansion** — land with 2–3 pilot vessels per operator, then expand fleet-wide as documented case studies with real detection numbers and false alarm rates close enterprise contracts
- **Data flywheel retention** — every deployment improves detection models; better models reduce false positives, trust grows, and AI updates ship only to active lease customers, making it harder to leave as the system learns their specific routes
- **Conference and trade press presence** — bridge command unit demos at Posidonia, Nor-Shipping, and SMM plus targeted coverage in TradeWinds and Lloyd's List puts Aegis in front of fleet operations executives and marine insurers where they actually gather
