## The Solution

- **A disposable swarm, not one expensive drone.** 10–20 low-cost underwater drones form a real-time 2 km protective perimeter around a vessel. If one is lost, the swarm recalibrates — the rest keep working. An entire swarm costs less than 10% of a single military-grade AUV.
- **Real-time alerts on the bridge.** Drones relay acoustic imaging back to a command unit on the ship. Heavy AI processing runs on-board. When a hazard is detected, the crew gets an immediate alert and can take manual control of individual drones to investigate.
- **No cranes, no ROV pilots, no $250K gamble.** Drones store in a compact launch-and-recover rack on deck. Deploy in minutes. Multiple swarms can be loaded on the same ship. Swarm size adjusts based on route risk.
- **Leasing, not selling.** $8K–15K/month per vessel covers everything — hardware, software, maintenance, replacements. One month of Aegis Swarm costs less than a single day of a survey vessel. Turns a capital decision into an operational one.
