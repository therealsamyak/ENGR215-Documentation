## The Problem

- **Ships are blind on their sides.** Hull-mounted sonar looks forward and down — zero lateral visibility for submerged hazards even a kilometer away. Whales, debris, UXO — detected only after impact.
- **One grounding can freeze global trade.** The *Ever Given* blocked the Suez Canal for six days — $9.6 billion in losses per day, hundreds of millions in direct costs to the ship owner. Insurance pays for repairs, not the contracts you lost.
- **No affordable solution exists.** Survey vessels run $20K–50K/day. Traditional AUVs cost $250K+ and operators hesitate to deploy them in the exact hazards they bought them to scout.
- **Every transit through a chokepoint is a bet.** Suez, Malacca, Panama, Bosporus — congested, poorly charted, and traversed daily by vessels worth hundreds of millions carrying 80% of global trade.
