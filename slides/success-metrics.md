## Success Metrics

### Prototype Validation

- Complete controlled-water trial with 6-drone swarm detecting submerged targets at 1 km range
- Achieve <5% false positive rate on synthetic hazard classification in simulation environment

### Pilot Deployment

- Deploy on 2–3 commercial vessels with active bridge-command alerts during real transits
- Reach 90%+ swarm uptime across a full 30-day pilot period with zero undetected hazards

### Market Traction

- Sign first enterprise lease contract (annual, multi-vessel) with a fleet operator or insurer partnership
- Convert 2+ pilot deployments to paid annual contracts within 60 days of pilot completion
