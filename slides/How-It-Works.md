## How It Works

### Swarm Perimeter Scouting

10–20 low-cost drones deploy from a deck-mounted rack and fan out into a 2 km acoustic perimeter around the vessel — self-healing, so losing one drone doesn't break coverage.

### Real-Time Bridge Command

Acoustic imaging relays back to a bridge command unit where AI flags hazards as instant alerts. Crew can take manual remote control of any drone to investigate, then release it back to autonomous scouting.

### Built-In Redundancy

Each drone is designed so the mission never depends on any single unit — the whole swarm costs less than 10% of a single military AUV. Flat monthly lease covers hardware, software, and replacements, so coverage never stops.
